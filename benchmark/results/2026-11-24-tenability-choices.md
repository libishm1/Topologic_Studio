# The tenability quantities: choices made where plan 16.54 is silent

Written 2026-11-24, before the tool read any result file. Plan 16.54 fixes the two quantities (`headCrossing60`, `headHeatDose`). Where it leaves something open, the reading nearest to how the head-height temperature is reduced (`tools/calibration/lib/head.mjs`, `validation-suite.mjs`) is taken. Each choice below was fixed before any number was computed and is not changed afterwards.

1. **The compared times.** As `head.mjs`: the test's measured times t with 1 s <= t <= the run's horizon, where the device has a reading and the model has at least one 1 s reading in (t - s/2, t + s/2], s being the median measured interval. Times are the data file's, zero at ignition. The model's value at t is the mean of those 1 s readings, less 273.15.
2. **The model's cell.** As `head.mjs`: the stored head cell nearest the device's height, left out when it is more than a cell away or above a tree's end (13.22). The tool checks itself against `headPairs` on every run: the peak rise it reads from its own series must equal the stored reducer's pair for every device.
3. **The crossing.** The first compared sample at or above the level; the time by a straight line from the sample before it. If the first compared sample is already at or above the level, the crossing is that sample's time.
4. **The dose.** `heatRate` of the app's `tenability.js` (T^3.4 / 5e7 a minute, 0 at or below 0 °C), summed by the trapezium rule over the compared times, zero at the first compared sample. Its crossing of 0.3 is found by a straight line on the summed dose.
5. **"Within three measured intervals of the start."** The start is the first compared sample. A pair is left out when the measured crossing is at or before the first compared time plus three times s. The count is reported ("unresolved").
6. **A miss whose measured crossing is that early stays a miss.** 16.54 leaves out pairs, and a miss is no pair. The share of misses is misses over the devices where the measurement crosses: the pairs, the unresolved pairs and the misses. The share with the unresolved and the early misses taken out of both is reported beside it and decides nothing.
7. **A model crossing at the first compared sample** is kept in a pair when the measured crossing is resolved. The count is reported.
8. **The experimental scatter.** The statistic needs a sigma_E and the guide gives none for a time. The head-height temperature's 0.07 is used.
9. **The three readings.** 16.54 names "pooled over measurements, each series weighted alike, and with or without the tests whose door the grid closes". The suite's code has: the unweighted statistic (`statistics`), the guide's 10-bin weighted statistic (`weightedStatistics`, weighted over bins of the measured value, not over series), each on every run (`stats`) and with the 2026-11-12 rule (`statsRule`). So the code has no reading that weights the series alike. Computed here, all six:
   - weighting: (a) unweighted, (b) the suite's 10-bin weighted, (c) the series weighted alike, which is the suite's weighted statistic with each pair's weight n over the pairs in its series in place of its bin;
   - runs: every run, and the runs the file marks as represented (`run.representation`, as stored; if a file has none, every run is kept and that is said).
   The three readings reported as 16.54's are (a) on every run, (c) on every run, and (a) with the rule. (b) is reported beside them. The strictest word is taken over all six.
10. **The word, by 16.54's rule, in each reading.** "In band" is the suite's calibrated band (`tier`: delta 0.8 to 1.25, sigma_M at most 0.35, at least two pairs).
    - A series is in band when its own statistic is in band and none of its runs on that cell stopped (the suite's `inBand`). In reading (b) the series' own 10-bin weighted statistic is used; in (a) and (c) its unweighted one.
    - "Most series that have pairs": more than half of the series with at least one pair.
    - Calibrated: pooled in band, most series in band, misses at most 5 %. Indicative: pooled in band and misses at most 5 %. Otherwise not calibrated. With fewer than two pooled pairs there is no statistic: "too few pairs", ranked with not calibrated.
    - If no device's measurement crosses, the share of misses is undefined and the word is "no crossing measured".
11. **Stopped runs.** Their devices are counted as "stopped" and enter no outcome. A series with a stopped run on the cell is out of band, as in the suite.
12. **Steady tests** (Steckler) are left out whole. FSE has no tree, so no device. The series that can have pairs are NBS, NIST/NRC, WTC, ATF, UL FSRI and UL/NIST, and the chairs as the ninth.
13. **Part B.** Reported for each file: the head-height temperature's delta over the same devices (peak rise, from the tool's own series), the two quantities' delta, the median of the model's time over the measured time, and the median of the model's summed dose over the measured one at the last compared time, over the devices where both are above zero. No band.
14. **Which 13.B files.** Fixed after reading plan 13.27 to 13.31 and before any computing: on 0.20 and 0.34 m the default-leak file and the sealed file, both, since 13.27's words are the strictest of the two; on 0.27 m the two files with 13.22 merged (`-pockets`, `-sealed-pockets`, plan 13.31), with the two files before 13.22 beside them. The ninth series for 13.B is `2026-11-13-validation-fsri-chairs.json`, joined in memory where it holds the cell.
